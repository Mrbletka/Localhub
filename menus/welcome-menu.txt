1. PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------
The first screen every player sees after joining the world.

It has three jobs:
  - New players  : explain the hook in 5 seconds, then steer them
                   to their first action (found or join a colony).
  - Returning    : "Here's what happened while you were away."
                   A snapshot of the colony plus the top events.
  - Everyone     : the front door to every other menu.

Rule of thumb: get the player playing in 1 click. It is a
welcome mat, not a wall. Never more than ~8 lines of body text.


2. WHEN IT SHOWS (TRIGGERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  [x] On first spawn after joining the world.
  [ ] NOT on respawn after death (that gets its own small toast).
  [ ] NOT on dimension change.

  Re-open anytime with:
    - the Colony Ledger item (given on first join)
    - a command, e.g. /scriptevent colony:menu welcome

  Bedrock catch:
    A form opened while the player is still loading gets cancelled
    ("UserBusy"). Plan: retry every ~1s until it opens, up to ~30s,
    or wait until the player first moves or looks around.

  Player setting "Welcome on join":
    - Always
    - Only when something happened   <- default for returning players
    - Never (just show a short on-screen toast instead)


3. VARIANTS (WHO SEES WHAT)
--------------------------------------------------------------
The menu picks one variant from the player's profile.

  A) FIRST JOIN         never joined this world before
  B) RETURNING LEADER   has a colony (Governor role)
  C) RETURNING MEMBER   lives in someone else's colony
  D) WANDERER           returning, but has no colony
                        (left it, was exiled, or the colony fell)
  +  ADMIN EXTRA        adds a "World Admin" button for ops/host

  Notes:
    - "Join a Colony" is hidden if no colonies exist yet.
      (ActionForm buttons can't be greyed out, only hidden or

    - Closing with X = same as "Explore First".


--- B) RETURNING LEADER ------------------------------------- [mockup]

  Title : Welcome back, Governor <name>

            Oakvale - Day 34, Late Spring
            Population 14 | Mood: Content | Food: 6 days
          WHILE YOU WERE AWAY (2 days)
            + Mira the farmer had a baby (Tobin)
            ~ A trader caravan came and left
            ! A zombie raid damaged the east wall
          (max 3 events, most important first, full list in Chronicle)

  Buttons:
    [arrow]   Continue          close the menu, get playing
    [hall]    Colony Overview   -> menus/colony-overview.txt
    [scroll]  Chronicle         -> menus/chronicle.txt
    [bell]    Alerts (2)        -> menus/alerts.txt   (only if any)
    [gear]    Settings          -> menus/settings.txt

  Colour code for events:
    green  +  good  (births, new settlers, finished buildings)
    yellow ~  neutral (traders, weather, seasons)
    red    !  bad   (raids, deaths, starvation, fire)


--- C) RETURNING MEMBER -------------------------------------

  Same as B, but:
    - Title  : Welcome back, <role> <name> of <colony>
               e.g. "Welcome back, Steward Alex of Oakvale"
    - Shows only what this role can see (open question, see 8)
    - No admin buttons for the colony


--- D) WANDERER ---------------------------------------------

  Title : Welcome back, Wanderer
  Body  : If the colony fell: "Oakvale fell in Year 2, Winter.
          Its story lives on in the Chronicle."
  Buttons:
    Found a New Colony
    Join a Colony
    Read the Chronicle of <old colony>   (only if it fell)
    Explore

6. LOOK & FEEL
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Option 1: Vanilla ActionForm   <- START HERE
    + works everywhere (phone, console, PC), easy to change
    + supports title, body text, colour codes, buttons with icons
    + buttons can have a 2nd line (a grey subtitle)
    - looks like every other addon menu

  Option 2: Custom JSON UI skin (later, polish phase)
    + our own frame, banner art, colony crest, 2-column layout
    - fragile: Minecraft updates can break custom JSON UI

  Plan: design content for Option 1 now, keep it reskinnable.

  Icons needed (16x16 textures, list grows -> assets/icons.txt):
    flag, people, book, compass, arrow, town hall,
    scroll, bell, gear, crown (admin)

  Sound: soft page-turn or bell when the menu opens.


7. DATA THIS MENU NEEDS (DEPENDENCIES)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  From PLAYER PROFILE (-> systems/player-profile.txt, planned)
    - firstJoinAt, lastSeenAt
    - colonyId, role (Governor / Steward / Citizen / none)
    - setting: welcomeOnJoin

  From COLONY (-> systems/colony.txt, planned)
    - name, day, season, population, average mood, food days

  From CHRONICLE (-> systems/chronicle.txt, planned)
    - events since lastSeenAt, each with an importance score

  From ALERTS (-> systems/alerts.txt, planned)
    - open problems (low food, no beds, under attack...)

  !! "While you were away" only works if the colony keeps simulating
     (or catches up) while the player is offline or far away.
     -> big topic for systems/simulation.txt
