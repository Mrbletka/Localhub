1. WHAT THIS MENU IS
--------------------------------------------------------------
The front door of HEARTHHOLD, a multiplayer colony simulator for
Minecraft Bedrock. Every player sees it after joining, and every
other menu starts from it and returns to it.

  The game in one line:
    Grow a floating-island colony on three pillars,
    WOOD · STONE · WATER (lumber, mining, fishing),
    alone or with up to 4 Mayors, next to other colonies on the
    same server.

  Words we use (keep them consistent everywhere):
    Mayor       a player who owns or co-owns a colony
    Settler     a colonist (NPC) who works, eats, sleeps, has kids
    Rathouse    the town hall; its level gates the colony's growth
    Colony      one floating island + its settlers + its Mayors
    Coins       the everyday currency, earned by playing
    Acorns      the rare currency (see open questions)


2. WHAT WE KEPT FROM THE BETA, AND WHAT WE CHANGED
--------------------------------------------------------------
  KEPT (this is the soul of HEARTHHOLD)
    + Wood/stone textured logo, the yellow splash text
    + "Wood · Stone · Water" as the identity of the game
    + The floating island as the hero picture
    + Big PLAY with session context and a privacy dropdown
    + Daily reward + daily quests + timed event banner
    + Open colonies with slots, privacy and online count
    + Player level, prestige, XP · coins and acorns
    + Controller shortcuts in the footer

  CHANGED (to make it more advanced)
    1. Zones instead of a collage.
       The beta had ~10 regions of equal weight. Now there are 4
       zones with one job each: ACT (left) · SEE (centre) ·
       KNOW (right) · MEET (bottom). See section 4.
    2. Materials mean something.
       Wood = do, Stone = go, Night glass = know, Magenta = live,
       Gold = earn. A player learns it once and reads every
       future menu instantly. See section 8.
    3. The island tells the news.
       Numbered pins on the island match the numbered list in
       "Since you left". You SEE where things happened.
    4. The colony panel is docked, not floating with a pointer
       line over the art, and it leads with ONE action
       ("2 settlers are idle > Assign").
    5. The avatar moved INTO the island. You stand at your own
       Rathouse door with a name tag. The centre of the screen
       now shows the colony instead of a large idle character.
    6. "$12,480" became "12,480" with a coin icon. A dollar sign
       breaks the medieval world.
    7. Open colonies shrink to one strip when you already have a
       colony, and become a main option when you don't.
    8. Same layout, different content for every kind of player
       (first join, returning, co-Mayor, wanderer). See section 7.


3. DESIGN GOALS
--------------------------------------------------------------
  1. ONE OBVIOUS NEXT STEP. One wood hero button (PLAY / START),
     focused by default. A / Enter / tap always does the right thing.
  2. READ YOUR COLONY IN 3 SECONDS. Island + alert + 3 numbered
     events. Details are one click deeper.
  3. A REASON TO COME BACK EVERY DAY. Daily reward streak, 3 daily
     quests, a timed event, the next Rathouse upgrade.
  4. IT FEELS ALIVE. Settlers working on the island, glowing
     windows at night, a colonist asking for help.
  5. EVERY DEVICE IS FIRST CLASS. Controller, touch, mouse and
     keyboard (section 9).
  6. NEVER BREAKS. A plain fallback menu with the same actions.

5. THE LIVING ISLAND  (centre)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  What it shows
    - Your real colony: Rathouse, houses, farm, quarry, dock,
      forests, river and waterfall, day or night.
    - Settlers at their jobs (miners in the quarry, fishers on
      the dock, lumberjacks in the forest).
    - You, standing at the Rathouse door with your name tag.

  Markers  (-> _ui-kit.png, "Island markers")
    Zone tag      "Quarry · 3 miners"      what happens where
    Numbered pin  (1)(2)(3)                matches "Since you left"
                  gold = good luck, green = new life, red = danger
    Idle pin      (zZ) orange              settlers with no job
    Unknown       "? Unsurveyed forest"    something to explore
    Name tag      "OakMayor"               where you are
    Ghost blocks  white outline            what you can build next
                                           (first join: the Rathouse)
  Max 3 numbered pins + 4 zone tags, so it never gets cluttered.

  How we could build it in Bedrock (needs a spike, see 11):
    A) REAL CAMERA (preferred)
       While the menu is open, the Camera API flies the player's
       camera to a slow orbit shot of their actual island. The menu
       panels sit on the left, right and bottom, and the centre stays
       see-through, so the island is the real world, live.
       Pins = small floating marker entities placed at the event
       locations, only visible to that player.
    B) PICTURE CARD (fallback)
       A pre-made picture per biome + Rathouse level, with pins at
       fixed spots. Less magic, but works everywhere.


6. PANELS IN DETAIL
--------------------------------------------------------------
  TOP BAR
    Logo        HEARTH in wood, HOLD in stone, + axe/pick/rod
    Splash      rotating yellow line, Minecraft style. Examples:
                "Welcome back, Mayor!" · "Wood! Stone! Water!" ·
                "2× fish this week!" · "Hearth is where the home is!"
                Personal lines first ("Tobin says hi!")
    Currencies  coins and acorns with [+] (opens shop.txt)
    Profile     head, name, LV, PRESTIGE badge, XP bar
    Settings    gear icon button (top right, always)

  ACT (left)
    Event       magenta banner: name, effect, countdown
    PLAY        the wood hero. Line 2 = where you will go.
                Dropdown: Public / Friends / Private (who can join)
    Browse      all colonies · Join code: type a friend's code
    Tiles       Guide · Wardrobe · Top 100 (badges = new things)
    Today       daily reward streak (7 days, Claim) and 3 daily
                quests with progress + reward; reset time shown.
                A finished quest turns its reward into a wood Claim.

  KNOW (right)
    Colony card crest, name, Rathouse level, day, Mayors (x/4)
                ONE action alert, the most urgent problem with a
                button ("2 settlers are idle > Assign")
                Skills: Lumber / Mining / Fishing, level bar,
                number of settlers working it
                Resources: wood, stone, fish + what changed while
                you were away (green +320)
                Next upgrade with what is still missing
    Since you left   time away + 3 numbered events (match the pins)

  MEET (bottom)
    Tabs        Open · Friends · Nearby
    Cards       mini island picture (biome), colony name, LV, biome,
                slots (2/4), privacy (Public / Friends), Join
    Browse all  (Y)

    NIGHT  (dark glass)   KNOW   colony, quests, news, colonies

    GOLD                  EARN   rewards, coins, focus ring

  Colours
    Ink #0B0818 · Night #16102C · Text #F6EFE2 · Muted #B9B0D0
    Gold #FFCF4A · Gold dark #B5841C · Good #6CC35A · Bad #EF5350
    Idle #F39C12 · Water #4FA8FF · Live #E13CA0 · Prestige #8B5CF6

  Type
    Logo + PLAY  chunky pixel display font (mockup: Silkscreen Bold)
    Everything else  the Minecraft font in-game
                     (mockup: Pixelify Sans as a stand-in)
    Section titles   13, gold, UPPERCASE, wide spacing

  Scene
    Night sky, stars, moon, light rays behind the logo, pink pixel
    clouds below, small islets floating in the distance.
    Day version: same layout, blue sky (todo).

  Rules
    One wood hero per screen. Small wood buttons only for rewards.
    3px ink frame + inner glow on every panel. 4px grid.
    Status is always colour + number/symbol + words.


9. INTERACTION & DEVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Default focus  PLAY / START
  Controller     A Play · X Quests · Y Colonies · B Close ·
                 LB/RB jump between zones · Start = Settings
  Keyboard       Enter Play · Esc Close · Tab moves between zones
  Touch          all targets at least 48px, footer hints hidden,
                 the island can be tapped to open Colony Overview
  Motion         island bobs gently, settlers walk, splash pulses,
                 panels slide in (150 ms). Respect a "reduce motion"
                 setting.
  Sound          soft wooden "thunk" on open, coin clink on Claim


10. CONTENT RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Voice      warm, cheeky, short. Talk to the Mayor, not about data.
  Numbers    12,480 (comma groups, no $) · 2× · 2d 14h · 9 hours
  Events     max 3, most important first. Danger beats luck,
             luck beats routine. Each event names WHERE it happened.
  Lengths    colony name max 16 · event max 36 · quest max 20 chars
  Localize   all text in language files, plus ~30% spare room.


11. HOW IT IS BUILT (FEASIBILITY)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  LAYER 1  Plain ActionForm (fallback)         -> _fallback.png
           Same actions, sorted by priority, long list scrolls.
           Always works. Built first.
  LAYER 2  JSON UI skin over that form          -> _returning.png
           Draws the zones, materials, cards and bars.
  LAYER 3  Live island                          -> section 5
           Camera API orbit + see-through centre + marker entities.

  Spikes (small prototypes to do before we commit):
    tech/json-ui-spike.txt       can the skin place cards, bars and
                                 badges from form text?
    tech/island-camera-spike.txt can we orbit the camera around the
                                 player's island while a form is open?
                                 Are the chunks loaded? Does it lag?


12. WHEN IT SHOWS  (TRIGGERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  [x] On first spawn after joining the server/world.
  [ ] NOT on respawn, NOT on dimension change.
  Re-open: Colony Ledger item · /scriptevent hearth:menu
  Bedrock catch: forms opened during loading get cancelled
  ("UserBusy"). Retry every second, max 30 seconds.
  Player setting "Main menu on join": Always / Only when something
  happened (default) / Never. When skipped, show an ACTION BAR
  MESSAGE instead (one line above the hotbar that fades by itself):
  "Welcome back to Oakridge. All is well."
